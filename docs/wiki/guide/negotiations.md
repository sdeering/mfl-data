# Negotiations

The transfer market is where MFL comes alive. Agents own the players. Clubs need the players. The bridge between the two? Contracts, trades, and good old-fashioned negotiation.

Every player on a club's books got there because an agent and a club owner sat down and hammered out a deal. You're not just picking the best players. You're negotiating terms, setting conditions, and sometimes pulling off multi-player trades that would make a sporting director jealous.

In MFL, **agents own players** and **clubs hire them**. Understanding this dynamic is the key to every deal you'll make.
