# Players

Player 101

Every player in MFL is **completely unique**. From the moment they're minted, they come loaded with their own name, nationality, appearance, position, attributes, and hidden potential. Some are raw talents waiting to be shaped. Others arrive ready to dominate. Your job? Figure out who's who and build a squad around them.

Players grow, peak, and eventually retire. How you manage that journey determines whether you're running a dynasty or scrambling for replacements every few seasons.

**New to MFL?** Start with Identity to understand what makes each player unique, then work your way down.
