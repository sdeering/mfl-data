# Economy

MFL Economy

MFL runs on a full in-game economy. You earn currency by competing, spend it in the store, trade assets with other managers on the marketplace, and collect rewards every season. Everything feeds back into the loop: compete, earn, spend, trade, compete again.

The economy is the engine behind everything you do in MFL. Every role benefits from it, whether you are a club owner chasing trophies or an agent flipping players for profit.

![](https://guide.playmfl.com/assets/mflcoinfinal.png)

$MFL, the in-game currency

## The Building Blocks

| System | What It Does |
| --- | --- |
| [**$MFL**](https://guide.playmfl.com/economy/mfl-coin) | The in-game currency. Earned through competition and contracts. Spent in the store and on entry fees. |
| [**MFL Store**](https://guide.playmfl.com/economy/store) | Where brand new assets enter the game. Packs for $MFL, packs for cash, and club packs for new owners. |
| [**Marketplace**](https://guide.playmfl.com/economy/marketplace) | Where managers buy and sell players, clubs, and packs for real money. Everything you own has real value. |
| [**Rewards**](https://guide.playmfl.com/economy/rewards) | How $MFL flows into the game through league placements, cup runs, and revenue sharing. |

Whether you are grinding leagues for $MFL, flipping players on the marketplace, or building a club empire funded by smart contracts, the economy touches every decision you make.
