# Rapid Play

Rapid Play covers the shorter-format tournaments and quick-fire events that run outside (or alongside) the main league season. Think of these as the side quests of MFL.

## How It Works

Rapid Play events are designed to be fast. Quick knockouts and results in days rather than weeks. They pop up during timed events and give you something to compete in beyond the regular league and cup schedule.

- Entry may be **free** or require a **$MFL fee**
- Some tournaments have **OVR-based entry requirements**

Always check the event details before entering so you know what you're getting into.
