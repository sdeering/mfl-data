## Owners and Agents

Club Owner

The core MFL experience. Acquire a club, build a squad, set tactics, register for leagues, and compete for glory and rewards. Scouting, transfers, formations, energy management — it all falls on you.

Players' Agent

A lighter role. Own players and place them at clubs through contract negotiations, earning a share of any rewards those clubs generate. Participate in the economy without managing a full club.

Most managers are both. Running a club while owning your own players is the most common approach.

## Common Questions

How does revenue share work?

When you place a player at a club, the contract includes a revenue share percentage. If that club earns rewards from competing, your player gets a cut. Higher revenue share attracts better players, but it costs the club more. It is a real negotiation.

Do I have to watch matches live?

Nope. Matches simulate automatically based on your tactics, lineup, and player attributes. You can check results whenever you want.

How long is a season?

Six weeks. That includes a regular season of league matches, followed by playoffs for promotion and relegation. Then a new season begins.

Do I need to know anything about blockchain?

No. The blockchain runs entirely in the background. You interact with MFL like any other game. Your wallet is created during signup and funded with a regular credit card.
