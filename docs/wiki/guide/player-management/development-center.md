# Development Center

Not every player has a club. Maybe you're holding a prospect you're not ready to sign to your squad. Maybe the market is dry. Maybe you just want steady, predictable growth without the chaos of match schedules. That's what the Development Center is for.

---

## How It Works

The Development Center is a solo training program for **free-agent players** -- those without an active club contract. You send your player in, choose a commitment length, pay the fee, and they earn XP at a flat daily rate until the term ends.

No matches required. No tactics to set. Just consistent, reliable development.

---

## Key Rules at a Glance

| Rule | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Duration** | 1 to 4 seasons. Longer commitments earn XP at a higher rate per season. |
| **Cost** | Scales with player OVR and duration. Higher-rated players cost more. Think of it as tuition. |
| **XP Rate** | Depends on duration. Ranges from 60% (1 season) up to 120% (4 seasons) of a club player's potential XP. |
| **Age Limit** | Players exit at **age 30**. The program is for development, not maintenance. |
| **Tier Restrictions** | **Legendary** and **Ultimate** tier players are **not eligible**. |
| **Match Limit** | Players who have played **more than 3 official matches** with a club during the current season are **not eligible**. |
| **Enrollment Deadline** | Must enroll **before the mid-season transfer window**. Miss it and you wait a full season. |

The longer you commit, the better the XP rate. A 1-season enrollment earns about **60%** of what a club player would get, but a 4-season commitment pushes that to **120%**. The tradeoff is liquidity: your player is locked for the full duration. No missed match selections or benchings. For young prospects without a squad place, that consistency is worth a lot.

---

## The Lock-In

**Once enrolled, your player is fully locked.** They cannot be sold, traded, or signed to a club until the term ends. Full stop. Make sure you're comfortable with the commitment before you hit confirm. A 4-season lock-in means that player is off the market for a long time.

---

## Choosing Your Duration

| Duration | XP Rate | Tradeoff |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1 season** | 60% | Maximum flexibility, but the lowest development rate. |
| **2 seasons** | ~80% | Better development while keeping a reasonable lock-in window. |
| **3 seasons** | ~100% | Matches what a club player would earn. Serious commitment. |
| **4 seasons** | 120% | Best possible rate, even surpassing club development. Full lock-in. |

---

## What It Guarantees (and What It Doesn't)

The Development Center guarantees your player **stays on track toward their potential**. They won't stagnate. They won't fall behind from inactivity.

What it does NOT guarantee is specific OVR increases. Your player will grow, but how much and where depends on the same progression systems everyone else uses. There's no cheat code here -- just a safety net.

---

## When to Use It

The Development Center is best for:

- **Young free agents** you believe in but can't sign to a club yet
- **Prospects you want to develop** while your squad is full
- **Players in a dead market** where no good club options exist

It's not the fast track. It's the safe track. Use it wisely.
