# Identity

Every player in MFL has a unique identity generated the moment they're minted. Think of it as their permanent DNA -- the foundation everything else builds on.

![MFL player personal information](https://guide.playmfl.com/assets/sdhgsdhsdh.png)

Personal details are allocated randomly

## Appearance

Each player gets an **AI-generated portrait**. These are purely cosmetic and have zero impact on gameplay. They're just there to give your squad some personality and make your squad feel alive.

## Name, Nationality & Physical Traits

When a player is created, the game randomly assigns them:

| Trait | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | Pulled from real-world naming data |
| **Nationality** | Tied to real countries |
| **Age** | Players in drops range from 16 to 28, with the majority being 23-24 or younger. Determines where they are in their career arc. |
| **Height** | Average is 181cm, max is 206cm. Minor gameplay impact on aerial duels and goalkeeper reaching ability. |

**Height matters (a little).** Taller players have slight advantages in aerial duels and goalkeepers benefit from extra reach. Nationality is purely cosmetic and affects nothing mechanically.

## Position & Preferred Foot

Every player is assigned:

- **One primary position** -- this is where they perform best, with no penalties applied
- **Up to two secondary positions** -- they can play here with a small stat reduction
- **Preferred foot** -- left, right, or both

Primary position has a direct impact on gameplay. It determines how a player's overall rating is calculated and where they slot into your formation without penalties.

**Preferred foot matters.** Players tend to use their preferred foot more during matches, which can influence passing, shooting, and dribbling in certain situations. Keep it in mind when positioning players on the pitch -- a left-footed winger on the right side will cut inside more naturally, for example.

## What Actually Matters

Most identity traits are cosmetic or informational. The ones that carry gameplay weight right now:

| Trait | Why It Matters |
| --- | --- |
| **Primary position** | Affects OVR calculation and positional penalties |
| **Height** | Minor impact on aerial duels and goalkeeper reaching |
| **Secondary positions** | Determines where else a player can slot in without heavy penalties |
| **Preferred foot** | Players use their preferred foot more during matches |

Nationality and appearance are purely cosmetic.
