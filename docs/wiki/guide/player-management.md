# Player Management

Your players aren't static cards. They grow. Every match, every training session, every tactical decision shapes who they become. Attributes only go up over a player's career -- there's no decline until retirement hits. That means every day you manage them well is permanent progress, and every day you neglect them is potential left on the table.

![](https://guide.playmfl.com/assets/development2 (1).png)

Long-term success in MFL comes down to three things: keeping your players healthy, feeding them the right experience, and knowing when to push and when to rest. Get this right and you'll watch raw prospects turn into monsters. Get it wrong and you'll wonder why your 5-star signing still plays like a 3.
