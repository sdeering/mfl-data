# Club Management

Your club is yours. The badge, the formation, the tactics, the staff, the matchday plans. This is where you take a squad of players and turn them into something that actually wins football matches.

Running a club in MFL goes way beyond picking your best XI and hitting "play." You're choosing formations, setting instructions for every player, building match plans that react to in-game situations, and bringing in staff to help manage the whole operation. The clubs that dominate? They're the ones where the manager sweats every detail.
