# The 6-Week Season

Every MFL season runs on a **6-week cycle**. It's fast, it's intense, and every single phase demands your attention. Miss a deadline and you could find yourself auto-relegated with a voided squad. Let's walk through each phase so you know exactly what's coming.

**Registration is non-negotiable.** If you fail to register your club before the deadline, you are **auto-relegated** and all player contracts are **voided**. Late registration is possible, but you'll start with a **points penalty**. Set a reminder. Write it on your hand. Tattoo it on your forehead. Whatever it takes.

---

1

### Pre-Season

The calm before the storm. This is your window to prepare.

- **Transfer window opens** -- start signing players to fill or improve your squad
- **Player pack drop** -- new player packs hit the MFL Store
- **Club registration** -- you **must** register your club for the upcoming season
- **Set your tactics** -- prepare your starting XI, bench and match plans. Scheduled matches can be set up **14 days ahead**, with saves closing **one minute before kickoff**.
- **Prepare training** -- arrange [groups, coaches and intensity](https://guide.playmfl.com/player-management/training) before the daily sessions begin.

Registration and signing contracts are the two things you cannot skip. Miss registration and you're auto-relegated. Miss the transfer window and you're fielding an empty squad.

2

### In-Season

This is where it all happens. Matches, training, and constant squad decisions.

- **Daily matches** -- your club plays a league match every day. Some days are **double matchdays** when IMFF Cup fixtures run alongside league play.
- **Daily training** -- sessions run at **08:00 UTC**, using your saved groups, coaches, focus and intensity. There are **29 sessions** in a season. Players at 60% energy or below recover instead of earning training XP.
- **Squad management** -- keep an eye on **energy levels**, **rotate your squad**, and adjust **tactics** as needed. Running the same XI every day will burn them out.
- **Plan next season** -- pre-contracts open a few days after the mid-season transfer window closes.

The managers who stay active every day have a massive advantage. Check in, rotate, adapt.

3

### Mid-Season Transfer Window

A short but critical break in the action.

- **Two days with no matches** -- use this time to make moves
- **Sign up to 3 new players** -- reinforce your squad for the second half
- **Mid-season pack drop** -- fresh player packs hit the store
- **Pre-contracts begin** a few days after the window closes -- start locking in deals for next season

This window is your chance to fix what's broken. Short on forwards? Leaking goals? Three signings can turn your whole season around.

4

### Post-Season

The dust settles. Time for rewards and consequences.

- **Rewards day** -- $MFL is distributed based on your final league position. Higher division, higher rewards.
- **Playoffs** -- clubs on the edge of promotion and relegation battle it out in playoff matches. These decide who goes up and who goes down.
- **Rollover day** -- divisions are reshuffled based on final standings, and the next pre-season begins immediately

One season ends, the next one starts. The cycle never stops.

---

## Key Events Timeline

| Phase | Duration | Key Events |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Pre-Season** | ~1 week | Transfer window opens, player pack drop, club registration deadline, set tactics |
| **In-Season (First Half)** | ~2 weeks | Daily matches, daily training, squad rotation, IMFF Cup matchdays |
| **Mid-Season Window** | ~2 days | Transfer window (up to 3 signings), mid-season pack drop, no matches |
| **In-Season (Second Half)** | ~2 weeks | Daily matches resume, pre-contracts open, final push for position |
| **Post-Season** | ~3-4 days | Rewards distributed, promotion/relegation playoffs, rollover to next season |

---

## Survival Tips

- **Register your club.** Seriously. Auto-relegation is brutal and completely avoidable.
- **Don't ignore energy.** Players who play every match will burn out. Rotate your squad.
- **Use the mid-season window.** Three signings can save a bad season or push a good one over the top.
- **Start pre-contracts early.** The best players get locked up fast. Don't wait until the last day.
- **Show up every day.** Daily management separates the contenders from the pretenders.
