# MFL Store

The MFL Store is where brand new assets enter the game. This is not the marketplace (that is where managers trade with each other). The store is MFL itself selling fresh packs and assets directly to you.

## Pack Types

### Drop Packs

Player packs purchased with real money via credit card or Dapper Balance. Drop packs are released during scheduled drop events and contain a mix of players across different rarities.

The pack-opening experience is a genuine rush. Every player slot has a chance to upgrade. A common slot might contain an uncommon, rare, or even legendary player. You never know what you are going to get until you rip it open.

### Reward Packs

Single-player packs purchasable with $MFL. These are available in the store year-round, giving you a way to convert your hard-earned competition rewards into new talent.

### Club Packs

Want to own a club? Club packs are available year-round and come with a club slot in the Flint division (the entry-level tier).

Each account can purchase up to 3 club packs, so there is a hard cap on how many clubs a single manager can start from scratch.

## Pack Drop Schedule

MFL runs pack drops on a predictable cadence:

- **Pre-Season Drop** -- Happens at the start of each new season. This is your chance to stock up on fresh players before competition kicks off.
- **Mid-Season Drop** -- Drops during the transfer window, giving managers a shot at reinforcements while the season is still live.

Drops are announced ahead of time and run until stocks last. Keep an eye on announcements so you do not miss out.

Pack drops sell out. If you want specific packs, be ready when they go live. The best packs move fast.
