# $MFL

$MFL (MFL Coin) is the in-game currency that keeps everything moving. You earn it by playing, you spend it on new assets and competition entry fees.

## How You Earn $MFL

You can earn $MFL through competition and contracts, or receive it when using Quick Sell:

| Method | How It Works |
| --- | --- |
| **Compete** | Enter your club in leagues and cups. Your final placement determines your payout. Higher divisions and deeper cup runs mean bigger rewards. |
| **Quick Sell** | Exchange eligible players for the displayed $MFL offer. See [Marketplace](https://guide.playmfl.com/economy/marketplace#quick-sell) for eligibility and the irreversible transfer. |
| **Revenue Share** | Sign your players to contracts at other managers' clubs. When that club earns competition rewards, your players and staff get their contracted cut automatically. |

## How You Spend $MFL

| Spend Type | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Reward Packs** | Buy packs in the MFL Store to get new players. You become their agent and can sign them to clubs, develop them, or sell them on the marketplace. |
| **Entry Fees** | Some tournaments and competitions require $MFL to enter. |
| **Training Groups** | Permanently unlock extra groups for your club and upgrade their division bands. |
| **Coach Salaries** | Pay a seasonal salary upfront instead of giving the coach revenue share. Basic coaches are free. |
| **Development Center** | Pay fees to enroll free-agent players in the Development Center for steady XP growth. |
| **Club Branding** | Renaming, rebranding, or relocating your club costs $MFL. Higher-division clubs pay more. |

## Key Facts

| Fact | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Transferable? | No. $MFL cannot be sent to other managers. |
| Sellable? | No. $MFL itself cannot be traded or sent to others. However, you can use $MFL to buy packs, and the players and assets you get can be sold on the Marketplace for real money. |
| On any exchange? | No. $MFL has no external market value, but the assets you acquire with it do. |
| Supply | Unlimited. Generated through competition rewards. |
| How to get it | Earned through gameplay only. |

**Conversion rate: 800 $MFL = $1 USD in the MFL Store.**

This is how store prices are set. A Reward Pack costing 8,000 $MFL is equivalent to $10 USD in value.

Think of $MFL as your club's operating budget. The better you compete, the more you earn, and the more you can reinvest into your squad. Managers who consistently place well in higher divisions build up serious spending power over time.
