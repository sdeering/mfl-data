### Elevating the Game: MFL Alpha Season 0.4 Unleashed!

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*kteZD3KjAB5WZo_t818oHw.png&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

Get ready, managers! MFL Alpha Season 0.4 is upon us, and it’s set to be our most groundbreaking season yet. With the introduction of positional familiarity and the revolutionary Team Instructions, we’re elevating the strategic depth of the game to new heights.

### Positional Familiarity: Strategic Squad-Building ♟️

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*RUHgjUBnkQkVIwn4K36Mrg.png&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

Positional familiarity brings a new layer to your squad-building strategy and match day lineups. A player’s effectiveness is now influenced by their familiarity with their playing position, adding an extra strategic touch to your management decisions.

*Want to learn more? You can find all the info on that topic* [*in this article*](https://blog.playmfl.com/master-the-pitch-introducing-positional-familiarity-in-mfl-83ba126d72b3)*.*

### Team Instructions: Control the Pitch like never before 🧑‍🏫

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*v73KL9BtIpNUZiLRsE-76w.png&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

Team Instructions are coming, and they’re about to *transform* your managerial experience. Gain unprecedented control over your team’s behavior on the pitch. Adjust the newly available settings to tailor your team’s playing style to your vision. This is an absolute game-changer — giving you a chance to influence your team in entirely new ways.

*An article dedicated to Team Instructions is coming soon!*

### Season 0.4 Timeline 📆

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*5Q5dqdR-Sq5ncbrW9EH35Q.png&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

**🏆 Key events:**

- **The Transfer Window is open!** Time to put ink to paper and sign players to your team.
- The IMFF is hosting a **Preseason Tournament** for managers to familiarize themselves with all the new updates. Registrations are opening soon, so make sure to keep an eye out for that.
- The last **Player Drop** in this series is planned for **Tuesday, January 30**. Don’t miss out on the chance to recruit new talent before the window ends!
- League action will follow shortly after the preseason champs have been crowned. A new edition of the IMFF Cup will also take place!

*The above timeline can also be found on the* [*MFL app homepage*](http://app.playmfl.com)*.*
