### Season 10: A Special Campaign

One year ago, MFL emerged from beta. Ten seasons later, we’re closing out the year with our **biggest celebration yet**.

Introducing **Holiday Heroes** —a global celebration of MFL’s tenth season that will last a week, and feature the first-ever bonus cash prize tournament with **$10,000 in Dapper Balance** on the line. More competition. More rewards.

### 🦸‍♂️ Two Paths to Glory

**The Masters** — $10k in bonus cash distributed across five competitive tiers. This is the big one. Entry is earned, not given. Only the clubs who qualify through November and December will compete for the prize pool.

**The Classic** — Didn’t qualify? You still play. Free entry for all clubs who didn’t make The Masters. Compete for extra $MFL rewards and prove yourself.

Both tournaments run **December 30 — January 4**. Both have **fatigue ON** and **XP earning active**. This isn’t just about prizes — it’s a chance to develop your squad while competing at the highest level.

### 🎫 Earn Your Spot

Qualifiers start **December 1st**. They run daily through **December 22nd**.

Here’s how it works:

- Qualifiers drop throughout the day
- Registration is **first-come, first-served** — when your tier drops, move fast
- **No OVR limits** — bring your absolute best squad
- **No fatigue, no XP** in qualifiers — pure competition
- **Win your qualifier = ticket to The Masters**

Each club qualifies independently. Multiple clubs? Multiple chances.

### 🔄 Prepare Your Squad

Right before the competitions kick off, we’ll be opening a **special transfer window**:

**December 28, 23:30 CET — December 30, 16:00 CET**

- **3 signings per club** — make them count
- Qualified for The Masters? Fine-tune your lineup for the prize push
- Competing in The Classic? Strengthen your squad for the grind

Squad depth matters. Fatigue is real. Choose wisely.

### 📆 Key Dates

- **December 1 — December 22**: Qualifiers run daily
- **December 28–30**: Special transfer window (3 signings per club)
- **December 30 — January 4**: Holiday Heroes tournaments
