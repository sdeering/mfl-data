# MFL Players are Back in Packs

LucasFebruary 27, 20232 min read

Dear Agents,

---

### MFL Players are Back in Packs

Dear Agents,

Get ready for a new wave of excitement! Player packs are coming and this time, you’ll have more chances to score.

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*GBC52ivQOMVS6odlKXzzEQ.png&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

### Main info:

- **8 bi-weekly drops starting March 7,** then every two weeks\*\*.\*\*   
  250 base packs, 75 rare packs, available for purchase each drop, and 15 legendary packs every two drops
- Players aged **16 to 24**
- **Limit of 1 Legendary pack** per person across all upcoming drops in this release. 2 Rare Packs and 5 Base Packs per drop.
- **Packs start at $14.99** and all have a chance of containing a Legendary player

### More fun, more often!

We’ve revamped the player drops to give all existing and prospective Agents out there a chance to secure fresh new talent. Starting **March 7**, you’ll be able to experience the thrill of ripping packs and discovering your new protégés **every two weeks.**

**These smaller, more exclusive drops will afford more users the chance to start their MFL journey in the most exciting way possible: by opening packs and onboarding brand new talent into their Player agency.**

We can’t wait to get into the rhythm and bring the community the excitement of drops on a regular basis!

### Drop Schedule

We will be conducting a total of **8 drops of equal size every two weeks**, **from March 7 until June 13**.   
After carefully examining the demand, **we’ve decided to cut the total supply we’d** [**originally planned for Drop 1**](https://medium.com/playmfl/announcing-mfl-players-drop-8bc40ae706ee) **by half.**

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*nCDLD7stUJmyQ-8kf8cJUg.png&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

Drop schedule for this release

### Pack contents

Each release will have its unique set of base packs, rare packs, and legendary packs available for purchase. \*\*The pack prices and odds remain the same as previously planned.  
\*\*The players contained in these drops are **aged between 16 and 24.**

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*fBZvlOy8ZOoHLb2vR_1xfQ.png&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

Pack prices and contents

### Mark your calendars

The player drops provide an incredible opportunity to add more depth to your agency, and who knows, you might just find the next MFL superstar in your pack!

As we gear up for the inaugural League season, there’s never been a better time to get started with MFL. Join us on this incredible journey and get prepared for official competitions.

See you all on March 7 at 11 pm CEST | 10 pm UTC | 5 pm ET for the first drop!
