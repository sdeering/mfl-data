# The MFL Nations Cup 2026 Is Here.

MFL TeamJune 1, 20263 min read

48 nations. $25,000+ in Bonus Cash. One month of football. The biggest event MFL has ever run starts June 2.

The FIFA World Cup 2026™ is coming. So is ours.

---

## What's Happening

The MFL Nations Cup is a full international competition running alongside the real World Cup. Same nations, same format. 12 groups of 4, knockout rounds from the Round of 32 to the Final.

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fd13e14gtps4iwl.cloudfront.net%2Fu%2Fcontent%2Fnationscuphero-1780065833068.png&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

But here's the difference: **you** decide who manages the team, **you** get a share of the massive prize pool, and **you** can even help your favorite nation win throughout the entire competition.

Every nation's manager is elected by its shareholders. The manager picks their staff, builds the squad, sets the tactics, and makes every call from group stage to the final whistle. 48 nations, 48 managers, all chosen by the community.

---

## $25,000+ in Bonus Cash

The prize pool starts at **$25,000 guaranteed** and grows from there. Every nation earns something. Even a Group Stage exit pays out.

All prizes are paid in Bonus Cash (Dapper Balance), which you can spend on players and packs across MFL.

---

## The Nations Cup Drop

**Tue 2 June, 20:00 CEST.**

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fd13e14gtps4iwl.cloudfront.net%2Fu%2Fcontent%2Fseks-1780066502396.png&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

Every Nations Cup Pack contains players exclusively from the **14 brand-new nations** joining MFL for the first time. Ghana, New Zealand, Haiti, and all the others. And every player inside is **23 years old or younger.** Young talent only.

Packs range from the $4.99 Kickoff to the $499.99 Max. Buy shares to unlock additional Nations Cup Packs beyond the drop.

---

## Fan Rally: Your Matches, Their Boost

Fan Rally is new and it changes everything. Play matches with your agency squad, earn Rally Points for a nation you back. The nation leading the rally before a Nations Cup fixture gets a real OVR boost in that game. Up to +3 OVR per player.

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fd13e14gtps4iwl.cloudfront.net%2Fu%2Fcontent%2Ffanrally2__1_-1780306037035.png&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

Your gameplay directly impacts the outcome of the tournament. No fatigue, no impact on your regular season. Just pure competition for the nations you believe in.

Top rally contributors compete for 10,000 $MFL every round. The top 3 per nation take home Bonus Cash at event end.

---

## How to Get Involved

1. **June 2, 20:00 CEST** — Grab Nations Cup Packs from the drop and discover new talent.
2. **June 2, ~21:00 CEST** — Buy shares ($0.99 or 800 $MFL each) in the nations you believe in, earn perks, and cast your votes for manager.
3. **June 10** — Voting ends, managers set their squad up. The first Fan Rally action kicks off.
4. **June 12 onwards** — The Nations Cup goes live. Keep rallying for your nation(s) and watch it all play out!

Shares unlock perks along the way: badges, national anthems, custom pitches, a very special kit, extra squad slots, and even the ability to grab more Nations Cup Packs.

For the full breakdown of every mechanic, every perk tier, every edge case: **[read the comprehensive guide](https://guide.playmfl.com/nations-cup).**

The Nations Cup is one month of football where every share counts, every rally match matters, and every nation has a shot. Pick yours.
