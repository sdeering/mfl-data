### Leverage the power of AI

Using text-to-image AI tools such as [Midjourney](https://docs.midjourney.com/docs/midjourney-discord) or [Bing AI](https://www.bing.com/images/create?FORM=GENILP) can be very efficient way to create an amazingly detailed logo that matches your Club’s identity within minutes.

**There are many tutorials out there to get the best out of tools like Midjourney. One of them is brought to you by none other than valued community member and fellow Club Owner, Duff!**

**Check it out:**

Duff’s Midjourney tutorial for making your Club logo

**If you’re the reading type or want to go more in-depth, here are some additional tips to help you out:**

#### Step 1: Make up your mind!

Select the tool you want to use using the links above. For Midjourney, you’ll have to hop in their Discord server or add the bot to your own server.  
Know what you’re looking for in terms of branding. You’ll at least need to think of a main emblem, colors and an overall style before you proceed. Sadly, these tools are not (yet) very good at writing text clearly or accurately, but you can also let the AI know your Club name for inspiration.

#### Step 2: Prompting

You now have to give instructions to the AI in the form of a **prompt**. Prompt engineering is quickly becoming a whole discipline of its own, and is one of the trickiest parts in all of this. The Midjourney Discord is full of tips and tricks on how to get the best results, and a quick Google search on that topic will also yield many results.

You can, however, get satisfactory results pretty quickly with a bit of practice. Here’s the prompt I used with Bing AI to get the below results for my Cairo-based Club, Nile Delta FC on the first try!

#### **Full logo with text**

> *soccer club logo “Nile Delta FC”, realistic sphinx emblem, vector, egypt theme, black and gold with minor turquoise accents, historic, no photorealistic details, thick outline, sports logo, football club*

*Note: When using the Midjourney bot, you’ll have to type the command /imagine before your prompt. Also, it’s important to Upscale the image you picked before downloading it.*

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*mjzbWtKxMxLAarM9UqKFVg.png&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

#### Without text

Another option is to generate a logo *without* text, so that you can add it later and not have to remove anything. Here’s the prompt I used:

> sleek and modern design logo featuring a sphinx in a clean and geometric style, vector, egypt theme, black and gold with minor turquoise accents, historic, no photorealistic details, thick outline, sports logo, football club

In most cases, you can also give negative commands like “no text” as part of your prompt.

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*6RqHPe8TrnsitfuLBbzJ1A.png&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

### Time to edit!

Whether you need to remove existing text to add it, like in the first example, or only add text to your design, you’ll need an image editing app. Save your favorite new creation and throw it into a design tool like [Canva](https://www.canva.com/) or [Pixlr](https://pixlr.com/). I personally use Canva for most of my quick-editing work.

#### Step 1: Covering up existing text (if needed)

Use either the Draw tool or a shape to cover the existing text. Make sure to use the color picker (eye dropper icon) to draw over it with the same color.

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*cqnc2AmvYaQ0z_2bEzlYfQ.gif&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

Remove AI-generated text to add your own using apps like Canva

#### Step 2: Adding your own text

Use to Text tool to find a font that you like and tinker with the size, effects, and colors!

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*13poERqLnex-KYhUmyCCHw.gif&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

#### Step 3: Removing the background

To remove the background color, go to <https://remove.bg>, upload your newly edited image, and download it again! If the tool removed part of your image together with the background, you can use the brush to restore the missing bits by clicking on ‘Edit’, then ‘Erase / Restore’.

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*pPdIDdz22GK0CoyILKvOdA.gif&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

By following these guidelines and tips, you can create a name and logo that accurately represents your club and is recognizable. Remember to take your time and create something that you’re proud of, and consider using AI tools like Midjourney to streamline the design process.

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*izjX2A3TrQtK59ki9jZVTg.png&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)
