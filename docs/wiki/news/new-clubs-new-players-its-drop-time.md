### New Clubs, New Players: It’s DROP time!

It’s been a long time coming, and it’s time to get excited for new MFL club and player pack drops this offseason!

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*g8eSbAlT1g0jHl20xplzWA.png&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

### MEGADROP on Tuesday, October 24th

Imagine this: *NEW PLAYER PACKS + NEW CLUBS PACKS, ALL IN A DAY.*

We’re celebrating the end of S0.2 with a bang! On Tuesday, October 24th, prepare yourselves for an opportunity to snag both club and player packs during a single, epic drop day🔥

#### NEW DIVISION, NEW CLUBS

Ever dreamt of owning a club and starting your very own football empire? Say hello to the STONE 🪨 Division, the freshest tier in the MFL pyramid!

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*FaeU8QycqCsngGf6NaGEXA.png&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*pPSk4VhucbZTXDFkqGFeFw.gif&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

768 Stone Division club packs will be available while supply lasts (left) – The updated MFL pyramidal system (right)

**We’re releasing a limited supply of 700 new Stone Division club licenses in Club Packs. They’ll be available for purchase while supplies last.**

This exciting Club License drop opens up a world of possibilities for both existing owners looking to expand their portfolio and new managers eager to embark on their MFL journey with a clean slate.

It’s your chance to discover your club’s new base 🌍 and craft a unique identity for your team right from the start. This is your ticket to starting your MFL adventure and setting your sights on promotion to Iron and beyond in the seasons to come!

**🗒️\_Important Details\_**

- Club Pack price: $79.99
- Maximum of 2 per wallet
- Get ready to take part in the Alpha testing for Season 0.3!

---

#### NEW PLAYER DROPS

Exciting times call for exciting players! Introducing the **Starter Pack**: Priced at just **$5.99**, these packs are exclusively designed for new users\* looking to dive headfirst into the MFL action.

\*Users who have purchased 3 or less total packs in their MFL journey so far will be eligible to buy up to 3 Starter Packs from the Megadrop on October 24th, and until supply lasts.

Each Starter Pack contains 2 Common players, and here’s the kicker — both slots offer the chance for an upgrade to an Uncommon, Rare, or even Legendary player! For seasoned MFL managers, fear not; all other packs will still be available for your delight 🙌

**📅 The initial Megadrop on Tuesday, October 24th boasts a supply of 830 total packs:**

- 200 Starter Packs
- 500 Standard Packs
- 200 Rare Packs
- 30 Legendary Packs

Over the following weeks, leading up to January 30th next year, we have 7 additional drops planned every two weeks. Each time, we’re releasing 60 Rare packs, 160 Standard packs, and 100 Starter packs. And for those chasing legendary status, mark your calendar — 10 Legendary packs will become available every month.

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*-hLCQTKhqoWKo2ETRBnSeQ.png&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

The upcoming schedule of Player Drops

**🗒️ *Important Details***

- The Standard Pack replaces the Base Pack
- We’ve carefully adjusted pack compositions to reduce the number of Common players.
- Upgrade odds have been fine-tuned to make Rare and Legendary players feel even rarer and legendarier! 😅 Please see the new odds on the drop page
- Pack prices have been adjusted to align with the current and historical market value of players a little better
- Players will be aged 16 to 26. Only about 4% will be 25 or older.
- The drops will continue to take place on a first come, first serve basis. Set those alarms!

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*VA1nTglKBfHjF9gLKVrBOg.png&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

Don’t miss your chance to be part of the excitement and elevate your MFL game. Mark your calendar for the MEGADROP on Tuesday October 24th, and get ready for a thrilling kickoff to a brand-new chapter in MFL history!
