# MFL Season 4: Bigger Rewards and Smarter Gameplay!

LucasMarch 6, 20254 min read

A new season is upon us, and with it comes major updates. Season 4 is raising the stakes with higher rewards, smarter gameplay, and more…

---

### MFL Season 4: Bigger Rewards and Smarter Gameplay!

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*OWzMokKFocmbLg3XzH8bKg.png&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

A new season is upon us, and with it comes major updates. Season 4 is raising the stakes with **higher rewards, smarter gameplay, and more control over your club’s future.**

From a **40%+ prize pool increase** to **improved negotiations and an upgraded match engine**, everything about this season is designed to make MFL more competitive, more rewarding, and more strategic than ever.

Let’s dive into what’s new 👇

---

### 💰 A More Rewarding Economy

#### New Rarity Tiers: Limited & Ultimate

The player market is evolving with the introduction of **two new rarities**:

- **Limited (55–64 OVR)** → A new tier between Common and Uncommon, bringing more granularity to packs and rewards.
- **Ultimate (95+ OVR)** → The **most elite players in the game** now have their own rarity, making them feel truly special.

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*P83Dquanq9NmrjAc06Ag1Q.png&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

New rarity tiers: Ultimate (left) and Limited (right)

This change refines **pack compositions, player progression, and overall market dynamics**, creating a **clearer player hierarchy** while maintaining accessibility for all managers.

---

#### 40%+ Reward Increase & Revamped Prize Distribution

Season 4 sees a **massive boost in rewards**, ensuring that active clubs and players **reap the benefits of competition**.

- The **total prize pool is increasing by over 40%**, directly tied to player participation and market dynamics.

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*pBVnE-hZlkAfsWI4rd9fhQ.png&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

- **Cup rewards are shifting** to include **group stage match bonuses**, ensuring every game has real stakes.
- **A flatter league reward structure** ensures **clubs lower in the standings** still earn meaningful rewards while keeping strong incentives for promotion.

These changes are all about long-term sustainability, ensuring rewards scale with the economy, and making each competition more meaningful.

### ⚽ A Smarter, More Realistic Match Engine

The match engine has been fine-tuned for better realism and smarter decision-making, making every in-game decision matter more than ever.

#### Key Upgrades:

✔️ **Refined Shooting Mechanics** → Players now finish chances more realistically based on their positioning and attributes.  
✔️ **Defensive Improvements** → Fewer defensive gaps, especially on counterattacks and after red cards.  
✔️ **Upgraded Sprinting Algorithm**  
✔️ **Smarter Referees**  
✔️ **Adjusted xG Calculation**  
✔️ **More Realistic Ball Speed After Saves**

These changes should make **gameplay feel smoother, fairer, and more rewarding!**

---

### 🤝 More Control Over Player Negotiations

Season 4 brings **game-changing improvements** to contract negotiations, giving agents more flexibility and control over player deals.

- \*\*Set Revenue Share By Division  
  \*\*You can now adjust your revenue share demands per division (e.g., 5% in Silver, 8% in Bronze), allowing you to field more tailored and realistic offers from clubs across multiple levels of the MFL pyramid.
- \*\*Set a Minimum Matches Clause  
  \*\*Agents can now set a minimum appearance requirement in the player availability, adding clarity and reducing friction in negotiations.

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*RPbwplGkNkN-yefvIkZbqA.png&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

The new options when setting a player’s availability as an agent

These tools allow for clearer and more precise contract negotiations, ensuring deals are fairer for everyone and get closed faster.

---

### 🏆 Competitive Integrity: New Rules for Season 4

To **enhance competition and maintain balance**, we’re introducing **new rules** for Season 4:

✅ \*\*Minimum OVR Requirement Per Division  
\*\*Ensures clubs field appropriate quality squads at each level.  
→ *Find the minimum OVR ratings per division* [*here*](https://whitepaper.playmfl.com/game-mechanics/competitions/leagues#participation-requirements)*.*

✅ \*\*Forfeit Rules  
\*\*• Clubs with 5+ forfeits in a season will lose their eligibility for rewards.  
• Squads with 5+ players below 60% energy will automatically forfeit the match.

✅ \*\*IMFF Cup Seeding Adjustments  
\*\*Seedings now consider the top 11 players under contract instead of top 18.

✅ \*\*Multi-Season Contracts Removed  
\*\*Simplifying negotiations and making roster-building more dynamic.

These changes are designed to reward engaged managers while creating a better overall competitive balance.
