### Goal Rush: Score ⚽ Together, Score 🎁 Together

Something big is kicking off in MFL. For the first time ever, anyone can jump straight in and feel the thrill of managing a club — no entry fee, no wait.

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*Q5z3wgrsa0v-I3scg76MQQ.png&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

With the [launch of **Trial Clubs**](https://blog.playmfl.com/trial-clubs-a-new-door-opens-4b32546e6e50), every football fan can take their place on the touchline, build a team, and experience MFL firsthand. It’s the boldest step we’ve taken to open the game to more managers, more stories, more competition.

And we’re not marking the moment quietly. We’re celebrating it with a tournament built for everyone — a fast, high-energy event that puts alliances, goals, and rewards at the heart of the action.

---

### The Rush

For **48 hours**, the entire community comes together in a race against the clock. Fifteen of your favorite creators and community leaders will lead their own **alliances**. You’ll choose your side, play matches, and fight for goals. Every goal counts — not just for you, but for everyone in your alliance.

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*Ib1xngc4CesQJ0RZodtHog.png&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

- Every goal = **1 point** for your alliance.
- Points unlock **milestone rewards** for the whole group.
- At the end, the **top alliances** take home exclusive prizes, merch, and bragging rights.

Think of it as a football festival: two days of nonstop matches, leaderboards shifting by the minute, and communities uniting under one banner to push each other higher.

---

### The Details

- **Registration opens:** **Tuesday, October 7 until Sunday**
- **Tournament runs:** **Mon, Oct 13, 8pm CET → Wed, Oct 15, 8pm CET** (48 hours)

Matches are quick, **opponents scale to your level**, and there’s a **45-minute cooldown** between games. You can use one of your existing clubs or your brand-new **Trial Club** if you’re a new manager.

---

### New to MFL? What your Trial Club can do

- **Compete** in **league and cup** competitions during your free season.
- **Build your squad** your way: **sign player contracts** with other managers **or** acquire players via **Packs** or the **MFL Marketplace**.

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*XfsmCbeoRtHgfiom27ssXg.png&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

- **Set tactics & lineups** each matchday (formations, instructions, XI).
- **Earn rewards** from your results; they **stay locked** to the club and **unlock if you decide to upgrade (purchase) the club**.

---

### The Loot

Hit the milestones, and your whole alliance shares in the loot: Dev Center passes, $MFL, packs, MFL merch.

Climb the leaderboard and the top alliances get even more — raffles for merch, $MFL to fuel community giveaways, even a **custom pitch** with the alliance leader’s logo.

The rewards are big, but the story is bigger: the moment rookies and veterans go shoulder-to-shoulder.

---

### Be There

This community has always thrived on competition — and even more on collaboration. Goal Rush is both. It’s a chance to prove that when we come together, we can do more than just play the game — we can grow it.

**October 7**: registration opens. Pick your alliance. Bring your best. Score for your community.

**Two days. One rush. Be there.**
