### Your Club. Your Season. Zero Cost.

Ever wanted to manage a football club? Not tap buttons on a mobile game. Not simulate seasons in minutes. Actually *manage* a club.

Welcome to MFL.

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*w6N27YwioYC8KR--L89bHw.png&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

### This Is Real Football Management

You’re not playing against bots. You’re competing against real people who care just as much as you do about climbing the table, landing that crucial signing, or pulling off a tactical masterclass.

Every opponent is human. Every contract negotiation is with another manager. Every match matters because someone else is trying just as hard to beat you.

And for the first time ever, it’s completely free to start.

### What You Get

- **Your own football club.**   
  It’s yours for a full season. No trial period that ends after three matches. An entire campaign from opening day to the final whistle.
- **Real competition.**   
  League fixtures. Cup runs. Promotion battles.   
  Compete hard against thousands of other managers.
- **Build your squad.**   
  Sign players through contracts with other managers. Scout talent. Make transfers. Build something that reflects your vision.
- **Total tactical control.** Set your formation, pick your lineup, adjust your instructions. Win or lose on your decisions.
- **Real rewards.** Every match you win, every position you climb, you earn rewards. They’re waiting for you if you decide to lock in your club after the season.

### How It Works

Sign up. Claim a club. Your season begins.

From there, you’re a manager. You’ll navigate transfer windows, set tactics, negotiate contracts, compete in cups, chase promotion. Everything unfolds in real time with real stakes.

When the season ends, everything you’ve built is waiting. Make the club permanently yours and unlock all the rewards you’ve earned throughout the campaign.

Your first season is free. What you do with it is up to you.

### Why MFL Is Different

Most football games give you everything instantly. Max-level teams. Unlimited resources. Matches that mean nothing because there’s always another one in thirty seconds.

MFL is the opposite.

Scarcity is real. Success takes planning. Your decisions compound over time. The manager who outthinks you may well end up beating you.

It’s built for people who love football, not just football games. People who watch matches and think “I’d have set up differently.” People who want to test themselves.

This isn’t FIFA Career Mode. It’s not Football Manager against AI. It’s a living, breathing competition where every club is run by someone like you.

### Made For Your First Season

We know you’re new. The game knows it too.

You’ll get a guided start: checklists that walk you through the basics, rewards for learning the ropes, everything you need to feel confident before your first match kicks off.

By the time fixtures start, you’ll know how to scout, how to negotiate, how to set your tactics. You won’t be lost. You’ll be ready.

### One Season. Prove Yourself.

Football management has always been behind a paywall or locked in single-player mode.

Not anymore.

This is your shot. A full season. A real club. Real competition.

The new season’s starting soon. Will you be in the dugout?
