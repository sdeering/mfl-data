---

### **Make Your Move: MFL Season 6**

A new season is about to kick off, and with it comes a fresh wave of features that make managing your club sharper, faster, and more fun than ever before.

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*KYbF6UWqvFha0yZir8LGhw.png&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

Season 6 builds on everything we’ve learned so far, with smarter gameplay, a more fluid user experience, and a game-changing way to buy and sell players. Whether you’re here to scout, scheme, or score, there’s something in this update for you.

Here’s what’s new in Season 6 📈

### 📏 **Marketplace Offers**

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*YyIJBMIRGN4roabtagJPuQ.png&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

The transfer market is getting a major upgrade. In Season 6, you’ll be able to send offers to other agents , even if the player isn’t listed on the market. Make your move, name your price, and choose when the offer expires. Negotiate directly and land your target. More control, more flexibility.

> **Please note**  
> *- For now, offers are limited to players only, with club offers a little further out.  
> - This feature won’t be available at the very start of S6, but we’re working to launch it in the first half of Season 6.*

### 🌟 **Enhanced Simulation**

We’ve made targeted improvements to the MFL match engine, fine-tuning realism while keeping more consistency than in prior off-seasons.

- Goalkeepers behave more logically and avoid chasing harmless balls or fumble easy catches.
- Defenders mark smarter, switching less and tracking better.
- Long balls are now less overpowered.
- Free kicks produce more natural outcomes, with fewer blocked shots and more chances from distance.
- Attacking play has more variety: better angles, riskier passes, and a bump in successful crosses.
- Plus, smoother ball movement thanks to early groundwork for future spin physics.

### 🤖 **UX & Quality of Life Improvements**

You may have already noticed some of these small changes since the later part of S5. From faster loading speeds to smarter navigation, we’ve introcued dozens of tweaks across the platform:

#### **Competitions**

- View recent signings in your league with the new Activity Tab
- Quicker page loads mean less waiting

#### **Tactics**

- Minimum Appearances now visible from the Tactics screen
- Direct links to your next opponent
- Improved mobile drag-and-drop

#### **Clubs**

- Contract table now sortable by more fields (like player position)
- Much faster contract loading
- Club Bio now editable

#### **Matches**

- Your match playback settings are now saved and remembered
- Fullscreen toggle before kickoff
- Rewind now jumps back 15s with the left arrow

#### **General**

- Cleaner sorting logic across many pages
- Updated competition icons

Small changes, big difference.

---

### 🏢 **New Reward Packs**

Want better control over the players you pull? Reward Packs now come in 5-OVR bands, making your outcomes more focused and rewarding.   
Instead of just selecting a rarity, you can now choose a specific OVR range, so you’re not hoping for a lucky roll on a wider pool.

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*wXF7rC8QyyvSW78zpFsNAQ.png&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

This doesn’t change the core mechanics, it just gives you more control over the type of players you want to chase. The regular reward packs remain untouched. If you prefer the old packs, they’re still there. Needless to say higher-range packs cost more, keeping things balanced.

> *🏷️ Pack prices will be revealed on* ***Rollover Day****!*

This gives you a real choice over what you want to chase, and allows you to more closely target players that would best fit in your squad.
