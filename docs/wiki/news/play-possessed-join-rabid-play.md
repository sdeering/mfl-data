### 🩸 Play Possessed — Join Rabid Play

Play possessed. Survive the frenzy. Win it all.

### 🎃From Rapid to Rabid

You’ve may remember **Rapid Play** — the quick-fire tournaments where the action never stops. This week, something’s changed. The lights flicker, the crowd howls, and every tackle carries a little too much bite.

Welcome to **Rabid Play** — our Halloween special where the same Rapid Play format gets infected with pure chaos.

#### The action kicks off on Wednesday at 6pm CET — until Friday!

---

### ⚽ The Format

- **Knockout-only.** No groups, no waiting.
- **Sizes:** 8-team and 16-team brackets for paid entry Rabid Play, up to 256 teams for freerolls.
- **Instant starts:** tournaments appear on the competitions page, and as soon as one fills, it launches automatically.
- **Rounds** begin the moment the previous one ends.

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*MKEVsyTnEgN7UmqhxLqQag.png&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

### ⚠️ Entry rules ⚠️

#### **OVR Cap**

- Rabid Play tournaments **only consider the highest OVR player** on your entire squad. In order to enter, your highest-rated player must be within a certain range. For example, between 61–65 OVR.
- For the paid entry tournaments, there is no range, just a maximum.
- These restrictions apply at both registrations *and* match time (locks 5 mins prior to kick off).
- Both conditions consider face OVR only (on the player’s card), and ignore bonuses/positional familiarity.
- There will be tournaments in the following bands.  
  We may add more depending on demand, like a no-limit version!

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*j8AF8MFzuC3ZFPBc_We4og.png&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

#### Other

- Max 1 club per owner in any given tournament.
- As with all other unofficial competitions and side events, fatigue is disabled and XP/progression is unaffected.
- If you’re planning on changing your default tactics for Rabid Play, **make sure to use the Upcoming Matches feature in the Tactics page**! This allows you to set a tactic ahead of time for specific League and Cup matches — bypassing your default lineup and tactics.

---

### 🩸 The Twist — Rage Mode

#### Fill the bar 📊

Every team in Rabid Play has a **rage bar**. Fouls and cards fill it up — and as the bar climbs, your players start **losing control**…

- When the bar hits 100% and 200%, a random player on the team becomes rabid.
- When they’re rabid, players are faster. And stronger. And taller. And they never tire.

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*lPDb0y_d5sbJ7VJ70raJbQ.png&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

#### Seeing red 🔴

- When they get a red card, players aren’t sent off. No, no. The ref wouldn’t dare. They remain on the pitch and go rabid in a hurry.

---

### 🏆Survive and Win

Each Rabid Play tournament rewards you, whether it’s a freeroll or paid entry. The deeper you go, the more you earn.

$MFL, Development Center Passes, even a custom pitch and a special pattern for the Kit Creator… you name it.

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*ipkDrKkwI_oHTJZP_-4K9g.png&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

**Rabid Play runs Wednesday — Friday from 6pm CET**, with tournaments spawning regularly on the Competitions page.

Enjoy this one-off Halloween special — fast, furious, built for chaos!
