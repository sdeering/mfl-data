### Trade Your Players for $MFL

To spice up the offseason, we’re launching a limited-time event during which we offer you $MFL in exchange for your unwanted players.

Boost your $MFL reserves thanks to players who are no longer part of your plans, helping you prepare for paid competitions and the ability to purchase packs on the MFL Store!

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*x0rSAWxAhFevSTV3QDwoZA.png&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

### How It Works

During this event, you can convert players from your agency into $MFL, our in-game currency. This process is simple:

1. Visit <https://app.playmfl.com/trade>
2. Select the players you wish to part with (up to 25).
3. Complete the trade, and we’ll send your $MFL next Wednesday.

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*hC4szqHq8DSZ-lNjMrQlQw.png&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

📆 The event closes on **Monday, May 6.**

### Why a player trading event?

This special event is directly inspired by community feedback:

- **Community Request:** This feature has been suggested and request many times! You asked for more flexible ways to manage your players and we listened.
- **Strategic Depth:** Selling players for $MFL opens up new strategies for team management and squad-building, enriching your gameplay experience.
- **Market Dynamics:** Introducing this feature temporarily allows us to observe and analyze new market dynamics created by direct player sales for $MFL.
- **Pilot Testing:** This event serves as a crucial test to gauge interest and functionality before we commit to developing a full-scale, on-site Quick Sell feature.
- **First Integration:** We’re also using this opportunity to integrate Evaluate’s trading tool for the first time, paving the way for potential future functionalities that enhance your trading experience 👀

### Participate Now!

Don’t miss out on this unique opportunity to reshape your squad and refine your strategy. Head to our event page now to start trading your players for $MFL. Event ends Monday!

We’re eager to see how this event transforms your approach to agency management and look forward to your feedback, which will be invaluable as we consider the permanent introduction of similar features.

---

### FAQ

#### 📆 When does the trading event end?

You can participate until **Monday, May 6.**

#### 🧮 How much $MFL can I get by trading my players?

Head to [app.playmfl.com/trade](https://app.playmfl.com/trade) to view the players in your agency and their corresponding value in $MFL.

#### ❓What happens to the players I trade to the MFL wallet?

They will stay there and won’t be usable in competition or for any MFL activities.

#### ⌚️ When will I receive the $MFL I’m owed from trading my players during the event?

Trades processed during the event will be settled with $MFL sent to you on **Wednesday, May 8.**
