**Countdown to Christmas with MFL!**

It’s the most wonderful time of the year, and MFL is celebrating the holiday season in style with **10 Days to Xmas!**

Check out your own MFL Advent Calendar and uncover fun prizes each and every day counting down to Xmas🔥Along with the Advent Calendar, we’re bringing the festivities to the pitch with Boxing Day Clashes and the release of an all-new formation every day!

### **10 Days to Christmas**

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F0*ZmNmZHYTf93D1Rj8&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

Head to <https://app.playmfl.com/xmas> and begin your day by opening your Advent Calendar slot for the 10 days leading up to Christmas. These advent calendars are personalised to you and there is something to be won **EVERY. SINGLE. DAY.**

#### **So what’s up for grabs?**

- **New Formations Everyday**  
  New year, new formations! Unlock a new formation **EVERY SINGLE DAY** during the 10 Days to Xmas\*\*.\*\* And here’s the fun caveat — Not every manager will unlock the same formation everyday. Test them out in Quest matches and expand your tactical options!
- **Lottery Tickets**  
  Win Special Santa Lottery tickets — enter into our Big Boxing Day lottery. The more tickets you collect, the more chances you have to win one of our many prizes including a **RARE pack**, 10 Standard packs, 10 Starter Packs and 10 Common Players.
- \*\*And a bunch of fun Christmas surprises!  
  \*\*⚽️ MFL Players   
  💵 MFL Points   
  🏆 Entries into a Boxing Day Clash tournament  
  📆 Early access to the next Drop   
  🎅 A limited-time Christmas pitch

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*OacfUdjh5zo-dxw19C5YXA.png&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

### **Boxing Day Activities**

After celebrating a lovely Christmas, head back to the MFL website where the festivities continue until the New Year.

\*\*Boxing Day Clashes  
\*\*Got a Tournament Ticket in your Advent Calendar? You’re in to compete in one of 10 Boxing Day Clashes that will be hosted on December 26th!  
They’re quick-fire, winner-take-all competitions featuring 8 teams each with 200 MFL points at stake.

\*\*Boxing Day Lottery  
\*\*And yes, the big Boxing Day Lottery takes place on Boxing day as well, so make sure you to hang on to these Lottery Tickets, and tune into the MFL Discord to keep up with the latest 🎉

**Enjoy the festivities, and don’t forget to open up your advent calendar slot everyday!**
