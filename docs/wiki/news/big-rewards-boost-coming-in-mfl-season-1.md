# Big Rewards Boost Coming in MFL Season 1!

LucasOctober 24, 20244 min read

With Season 1 fast approaching, the stakes are reaching new heights. Players will age and develop, clubs will rise or fall through…

---

### Big Rewards Boost Coming in MFL Season 1!

With **Season 1** fast approaching, the stakes are reaching new heights. Players will [age](https://blog.playmfl.com/aging-retirement-a-new-dynamic-for-players-e37a5feb6f7f) and [develop](https://blog.playmfl.com/player-progression-is-coming-heres-how-it-will-work-a0f001fda0cb), clubs will rise or fall through [promotion and relegation](https://blog.playmfl.com/your-guide-to-promotion-and-relegation-836dea00be82) battles, and every decision will matter more than ever. Now is the perfect moment to bring in **bigger rewards**.

Expect fiercer competition, higher intensity, and much bigger opportunities for those who excel. This is your chance to push your club to new levels. Let’s explore how we’ve ramped up rewards and what it means for your team.

---

### How We Calculated the New Rewards 💡

We’ve taken a hard look at historical data and made **big changes** to ensure rewards are worth fighting for *AND* sustainable. Here’s what we did:

- We’ve gathered a wealth of insights from 8 Alpha Seasons, covering every corner of our ecosystem. From the historical value of assets to marketplace activity, and the hundreds of thousands of player contracts signed — we’ve **used this data to estimate the cost of building a team** in each division. With these insights, we’ve adjusted rewards to better fit the landscape as we head into Season 1.
- We’re ensuring the rewards remain slightly **deflationary**, meaning they’re designed to stabilize the supply of players while keeping our **main revenue stream — primary sales** — alive and kicking.
- Compared to previous seasons, the total reward increase is **5.20x** (April 2024 saw a 30% boost, now we’re multiplying by ~4). This means **more $MFL** will be up for grabs than ever starting from Season 1 🔥

### Why It Matters 🌱

We didn’t just boost rewards for the sake of it, and we didn’t throw out a random number — we’ve carefully designed this with sustainability in mind.

As players begin to age and retire in future seasons, deflationary rewards will help slow the introduction of new MFL players, gradually shrinking the overall pool. This should help stabilize player values and keep the economy healthy, while allowing us to continue selling packs, fueling the MFL machine.

**Strong performance will give you a real chance to maximize rewards and create value, with the system designed to reward success while keeping the ecosystem healthy.**

**To give you an idea:**

- Teams performing reasonably well will be able to use their rewards to **keep up with** **player retirements**, making sure they can stay competitive as the seasons go by.
- Top performing clubs will typically generate **surplus players** — with the option to keep them for further squad building or to sell them for profit.

*These projections are based on pre-Season 1 insights.*

---

### What’s $MFL Anyway? 🪙

**$MFL** is our internal currency. It’s earned by competing, and while it **can’t be traded or converted into cash**, it’s your ticket to getting ahead in the game. You can use $MFL to:

- **Buy new players** and strengthen your squad with fresh talent
- **Send free agent players to the** [**Development Center**](https://blog.playmfl.com/introducing-the-mfl-development-center-nurturing-free-agents-6c68f9ae2d97) and watch them blossom
- **Enter paid tournaments** and compete for a share of the prize pool

---

### Distribution Across Competitions🏆

- **70% of the total $MFL rewards** go to **league competitions**, so your performance throughout the season matters. Plus, Season 1 means your club [can get promoted](https://blog.playmfl.com/your-guide-to-promotion-and-relegation-836dea00be82), and therefore access higher rewards the following season.
- **30% of rewards** go to the **IMFF Cup**, the flagship cup competition in MFL.

This ensures that both **long-term consistency** and **cup glory** are rewarded.

### What’s Next? ⏩

We’ll be closely monitoring how rewards play out each season to make sure they continue to fit the needs of the ecosystem. **Adjustments will happen** as needed, but the goal stays the same: keep things **sustainable** and **rewarding** for everyone.

---

**Here’s the short version for those of you who eagerly scrolled all the way down:** We’re supercharging rewards for Season 1 —multiplying them by 5.2x compared to the first five Alpha Seasons — but in a way that keeps the MFL ecosystem strong and thriving.

Season 1 is almost here (*coming November 5th!*), and we can’t wait to see how you use these rewards to build your legacy.
