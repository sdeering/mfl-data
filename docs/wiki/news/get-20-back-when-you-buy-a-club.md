# Get $20 back when you buy a club.

MFL TeamMarch 26, 20262 min read

Season 13 kicks off shortly, and we're celebrating with the biggest referral promo we've ever run.

---

### The Deal

Buy a club on the MFL primary market during the promo period, and you'll receive **$20 in Dapper Balance**. That's $20 you can spend on player packs, marketplace signings, or anything else inside MFL.

But it gets better.

![Season 13 cashback promo](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fd13e14gtps4iwl.cloudfront.net%2Fu%2Fcontent%2Fhf_20260326_190212_3a59ebdc-ebac-4f6c-9387-048e382dca72-1774555044324.png&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

Share your referral link with a friend. If they buy a club, **you earn $20 too**. And your friend still gets their own $20 cashback from the purchase. You both win.

There's no cap on referrals. Bring 5 friends, earn $100. Bring 10, earn $200. Simple as that.

---

### 📜 **How it works:**

1. Go to the MFL Store and buy the S13 Promo Club Pack ($80)
2. You receive $20 in Dapper Balance within 48 hours
3. Share your referral link with friends
4. When a friend buys a club through your link, you both earn $20

---

### ❗**Important details:**

- The promo runs from **March 27 to April 13, 2026** (18 days)
- One S13 Promo Club Pack per account
- Only primary market purchases qualify (not marketplace)
- This stacks with the existing referral program. You still get the Common player on top of the $20
- Dapper Balance is non-withdrawable. It can only be used within MFL
- You need to complete Dapper identity verification (KYC) before your cashback is credited
- Fraud-flagged or duplicate accounts are not eligible

---

> 🪙 **What is Dapper Balance?**  
> Dapper Balance is a credit on the Dapper platform. Use it to buy packs, sign players on the marketplace, or anything else in MFL. Think of it as store credit. It cannot be withdrawn or converted to cash.

Season 13 starts on Monday. Whether you're a veteran manager looking to expand or someone who's been on the fence, this is the moment. Buy your club, grab your $20, and start building your squad.

And don't forget to share your referral link. Every friend who joins puts another $20 in your pocket.
