### **MFL Community Clash: Jump In and Win Big!**

Get ready for the **MFL Community Clash**! This invitational knockout alliance tournament is set to be a thrilling showdown, whether you’re competing or cheering from the sidelines. There’s plenty to look forward to, including a shot at some incredible prizes. With a massive 3**00k $MFL prize pool** (and more) up for grabs, alliances led by creators and community champs will be battling it out for victory — and you can be part of it!

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*TS7WYTCMAhTIMwyQTsHUhQ.png&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

### **What’s the Clash About?**

Dive into the Community Clash, an invitational knockout tournament where MFL creators and community leaders join forces with four followers. These alliances battle in intense matches, all vying for a piece of the massive prize pool. Not leading an alliance? No worries! There are still two exciting ways for you to get in on the action and snag some prizes.

#### **Win your Team a Spot 🏆**

Alliance leaders will hold giveaways to pick members for their alliances. Keep an eye on your favorite MFL creators on social media for a chance to score a spot on one of these alliances. If you’re lucky, you could be playing with your own team and competing for that 300k $MFL prize pool.

#### **Follow the Tournament and Win Prizes 🎁**

Not playing? No problem! There are still prizes up for grabs. As alliances progress through the tournament, creators will be hosting giveaways. Follow along, engage with their streams or content, and you could snag some great rewards just for tuning in.

Among the alliance leaders are MFL content creators, as well as some of the most active & supportive community members.

---

### 📆**Details & Dates**

#### Format

The Clash will follow a Davis Cup-style format. Teams must stay under 750 OVR Cap. In the Round of 32, pairs of alliances will face off, with each team competing against a random member of the opposing alliance. The alliance with the most points at the end of the 5 matches will proceed to the next round.

#### **Alliance Formation**

Alliance leaders must finalize their alliances before Saturday 26 at 9pm CET, with some running giveaways for spots during this time — stay alert!

#### **Tournament Dates**

Matches will run from 28 to 31 October, with fresh content and prize opportunities after each round.

#### **Prize Pool Breakdown**

The total prize pool is 300k $MFL, divided between winning teams.

- Winner: 100,000 $MFL
- Runner-up: 72,000 $MFL
- Semi-finalists: 26,000 $MFL
- Quarter-finalists: 11,000 $MFL
- Round of 16: 3,750 $MFL

Mark your calendars for October 28 and get ready to jump into the fun!
