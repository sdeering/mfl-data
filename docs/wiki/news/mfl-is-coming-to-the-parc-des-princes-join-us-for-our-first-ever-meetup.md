### MFL is coming to the Parc des Princes: Join us for our first ever meetup

Three years. That’s how long MFL has been alive and kicking.  
Three years of building, competing, and growing a football universe with one of the most passionate communities out there.

To celebrate, we’re bringing the game to real life, and not just anywhere:  
**This is happening at the Parc des Princes in Paris** 🇫🇷.  
Yes, the actual PSG stadium. Yes, fresh off their Champions League win. Yes, there’s a chance *that* trophy might be on site.

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*PLX_8_dyUBg9HgI8JpF6eA.png&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

### 📍 Saturday, July 5 · Paris, Parc des Princes

Join us in the French capital for an unforgettable day with the MFL team and community — including top managers you’ll know from the leaderboards, Discord and beyond.

*This celebration is in part made possible with the support of* ***Flow****, the blockchain MFL is built on. We’re grateful for their help in bringing the community together for something memorable.*

### 🔥 The Experience

#### **Saturday from 4:30pm**

You’re stepping into one of football’s most iconic venues, and we’re taking you pitch-side.

🔓 **VIP access and tour** of the iconic stadium  
 ⚽️ **Pitch-side energy** and challenges  
 🍸 **Private cocktail dinner** with the community  
 📺 **Watch party** for the Club World Cup quarterfinals  
 🎁 **MFL surprises** we’re keeping close to us for now

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F0*AD5MWdvbIArGzjSP.jpg&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

#### **Sunday from 11:30am**

☕️ Brunch near the stadium  
🤝 Chill. Connect. Share war stories with fellow managers.

---

### 🎟️ Secure your spot

Tickets are limited and only a few are left. Contact us via the help chat to enquire.

Already RSVP’d? Book your trip, pack your gear, and stay close to Boulogne-Billancourt to stay in the heart of the action.

***– The MFL Team***
