### Introducing Contracts in MFL: Power Up Your Game!

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*Bpzqbq2cDIgoWtjaW1yweQ.png&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

We are thrilled to announce the introduction of a game-changing new feature: Contracts.   
This feature, now in beta, will bring your gaming experience to a whole new level. As a Club Owner, you can now offer contracts directly to player’s agents. And as an agent, you have the power to negotiate on behalf of your players!

### 📝 Contracts — Let’s Get Negotiating

With Contracts, as a Club Owner, you now have the authority to offer contracts to the players’ agents. This is a major step forward in adding more depth to the MFL experience.

> To celebrate this new release, we’re launching a [**mini-game**](http://bit.ly/betatestandwin) for the rest of the month to give you a taste of this feature. You could get your hands on some *serious* prizes by taking part in the Beta 🎁🎁🎁

For the time being, only Club Owners can make contract offers. But don’t worry, Agents! You can still head to our lively Discord community to find Clubs for your players and negotiate there. Making a contract offer is as easy as pie — you can do it directly from a player’s page, from our new scouting tool, or from your HQ’s ‘Players’ tab if you’re making offers to players in your own agency.

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*N9IzeLDfJ57yb0tBeF-0Yw.png&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

Making a Contract offer!

Here’s a nifty aspect of Contracts — you can withdraw a contract offer before the player’s Agent has made a decision. And if the agent has already accepted your offer, you still can request to cancel it, but keep in mind, the agent has the right to deny your cancellation requests.

### 📒 Your Contract Command Center

To help you manage this exciting feature, we’ve added a “Player Contracts’ tab to ‘My HQ’. You can easily keep tabs on past and pending contract offers, as well as active contracts for players in your agency. If you’re a Club Owner, we’ve created a similar page in your Club interface, where you can manage your offers and track your contracted players.

### 🔎 New Scouting Tool — Find The Perfect Fit

Good news for Club Owners! With our new Scouting tool, you no longer have to sift through Airtable to scout players. You can now find the right players for your Club directly on from your Club’s management interface, using various filters, and even offer contracts to players right from there.

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*SsyLUs2iQIrxi-kHdPkQ4A.png&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

The Scouting Tool

### 💁 Player Pages — It’s All About The Players

We’ve set up individual pages for every player where you can access their personal info, attribute ratings, past transactions, and contract details. It’s your comprehensive resource for any player in the MFL ecosystem.

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*WG44oUOu5Swjmjuu5dpAlw.png&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

Dirk’s Player Page

### 📬 Inbox — Stay on Top of Your Game

We’re also rolling out the Inbox feature, where you’ll receive notifications about contract offers, player transactions, and more. You can manage messages, respond to any offer right from the inbox, and filter your inbox to only show messages related to a specific part of your operations.

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*mt3wHKzQNHjG9s6xNzQvjw.png&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

Your very own Inbox!

We can’t wait for you to dive into these new features and hope they’ll enhance your MFL experience. Remember, we’re still in beta mode for Contracts, so your feedback is valuable and much appreciated.   
Again, make sure to participate in the Beta for a chance at awesome prizes, which include a Legendary Pack, a Legendary Player, and a TON of other rewards.

Happy contracting,

*The MFL Team*
