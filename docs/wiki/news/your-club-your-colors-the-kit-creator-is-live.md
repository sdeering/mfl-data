### Your Club, Your Colors: The Kit Creator Is Live

Every great football club needs an identity. A badge. A stadium. And most importantly — a kit that makes your players unmistakable the moment they step onto the pitch.

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*zIm_EPO7Um1aIpVlr3L60w.png&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

Today, we’re giving you the tools to create it.

### 🎨 Design Your Club’s Look

The **Kit Creator** is now live for all MFL club owners, letting you design custom home and away kits that reflect your club’s identity. No more generic templates — your squad, your style.

Here’s what you can do:

**Full Creative Control**  
Choose from a range of patterns, colors, and designs to build kits that stand out. Your club logo is automatically applied, so every creation feels authentically yours.

**See It in 3D**  
The Kit Creator features a sleek 3D preview that lets you rotate and inspect your design from every angle as you build it. What you see is what your club gets.

**Home & Away**  
Design both your primary and secondary kits. Whether you want a bold statement or clean simplicity, the choice is yours.

### 👕 Where Your Kits Appear

Once you finalize your design, your kits go live immediately:

- **Club Page**: Showcased front and center for everyone to see
- **Tactics Screen**: Your kits now appear when you’re setting up your squad
- **Match Pages**: Watch your players wear your colors during every fixture

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*CeL2IhBwFZ0UoygnAnRzhQ.png&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

*Note: Kits won’t appear on official player photos or cards just yet — but that’s a feature we’re planning to give club owners control over in the future.*

### 🏆 Join the Kit Contest

To celebrate the launch, we’re running a **Kit Design Contest** starting right after this post drops.

**How It Works:**

1. Create your best kit using the Kit Maker
2. Submit it in the replies to our upcoming contest [tweet](http://x.com/playmfl)
3. We’ll select our 16 favorite submissions
4. Jimbo will host the final bracket live on the **pre-drop stream next Wednesday**

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*MN38VX9tKsgPCypz7Dlecw.png&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

### 📛 Access & Pricing

The Kit Maker is available now on your **Club Page** — just look for the option to create your kit.

**Important Details:**

- **Free for Season 9**: Create and change your kits as much as you want this season at no cost
- **Starting Season 10**: Creating or changing kits will incur a fee in $MFL (details coming soon)
- **Rebranding Fees**: Club rebranding will also require a fee starting next season
- **Your First Club Free**: If this is your first time owning a club (new or from the marketplace), rebranding and kit creation are free.

### Start Creating

Your club’s identity starts here. Whether you’re going for classic stripes, bold gradients, or something no one’s ever seen before — the Kit Maker gives you the canvas.

[**Create Your Kit Now**](http://app.playmfl.com)

And keep an eye out for the contest tweet. We want to see what you build.
