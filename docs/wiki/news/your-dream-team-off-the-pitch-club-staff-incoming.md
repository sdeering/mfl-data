# Your Dream Team, Off The Pitch: Club Staff incoming!

LucasFebruary 16, 20264 min read

The lone wolf era of MFL is ending.

---

### Your Dream Team, Off The Pitch: Club Staff incoming!

The lone wolf era of MFL is ending.

Club ownership has always meant shouldering every decision, every tactical tweak, every transfer negotiation alone. You’ve built something incredible, but what if you didn’t have to do it all yourself?

We’re thrilled to announce **Club Staff** — a feature that fundamentally transforms how you can run your MFL club.

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*Hh_662haOStcmXWGUzcTlw.png&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

### Building Something Bigger Together

Imagine this: You’re heading into a crucial League matchday. Your Assistant Manager is fine-tuning tactics while your Community Manager is crafting the perfect pre-match hype thread. Meanwhile, you’re closing a blockbuster transfer that will define your season.

For the first time in MFL, you will be able to invite other managers to join your backroom team. Up to 5 staff members can help you run your club, each with custom roles and responsibilities that you define. Want a dedicated scout to handle transfers? A tactical genius to manage your lineup? A social media wizard to build your brand? Now you can have it all.

### What This Changes

#### **For Club Owners: Multiply Your Impact**

Running a top-tier MFL club is demanding. Between tactics, transfers, competitions, and building your brand, there’s only so much one person can do. Club Staff lets you delegate without losing control. Set the exact permissions each staff member has, adjust them any time, and build the dream team that takes your club to the next level.

Going on vacation? Burned out? Managing multiple clubs? Your staff can keep things running smoothly while you’re away. This isn’t just about sharing work — it’s about amplifying what your club can achieve.

#### **For Curious New Managers: The Perfect Entry Point**

MFL can be overwhelming when you’re starting out. There’s a lot to learn, and jumping straight into club ownership is a big commitment. Club Staff offers a different path.

Join an established club as staff. Learn the ropes with experienced guidance. Get hands-on experience without the full responsibility or financial commitment of ownership. When you’re ready to fly solo, you’ll have the knowledge and connections to thrive.

#### **For Everyone: Play With Your Friends**

Sports are better with your crew. Now your MFL experience can be too.

Recruit your friends to your backroom team. Build a club culture that’s uniquely yours. Strategize together in your group chat. Celebrate victories as a real team. Create stories and rivalries that go beyond individual achievements.

### How It Works: Simple, Flexible, Protected

The system is designed to be straightforward:

- **Send an offer** to any MFL manager with a title, permissions, revenue share, and offer duration
- **Choose their role** from six permission categories: Contract Offers, Tactics, Competitions, Branding, Social, and Contracts
- **Choose their title** from preset options: Manager, Assistant Manager, Sporting Director, Technical Director, Head of Football Operations, Head of Recruitment, Head of Brand, Club Secretary, or Community Manager
- **Set their cut** — staff share the same revenue pool as players, giving you complete control over your economics
- **Set offer duration** — how long the offer remains valid before expiring
- **Adjust on the fly** — change permissions any time to respond to performance or circumstances
- **One season commitments** — all contracts last one season, with revenue share honored even if you revoke permissions

You stay in control. Your staff get real skin in the game. Everyone wins.

### Available Tuesday

Club Staff is rolling out tomorrow (Tuesday, 17th February). Get ready to head to your club’s Staff tab to send your first offer!

This is just the beginning. We’re watching how you use this system and planning future enhancements based on what we learn. As always, your feedback will shape where we go next.

MFL has always been about building something that matters. Now you can build it together.

**Your club. Your team. Your legacy.**
