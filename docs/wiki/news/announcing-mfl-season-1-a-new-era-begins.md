# Announcing MFL Season 1 — A New Era Begins!

LucasOctober 15, 20245 min read

MFL is officially exiting its Alpha phase and is stepping into the spotlight with the launch of Season 1 on November 5th.

---

### Announcing MFL Season 1 — A New Era Begins!

#### MFL is officially exiting its Alpha phase and is stepping into the spotlight with the launch of Season 1 on November 5th.

#### Mark your calendars: **Season 1** kicks off on **November 5th**!

After months of development and support from our amazing community, we’re stepping onto the pitch with our biggest update yet. **Season 1 takes everything you love about MFL and pushes it to the next level.**   
Whether you’re nurturing future legends through player progression, battling through the new promotion and relegation system, or maximizing rewards in high-stakes competitions, this season is set to raise the bar.

We’re bringing deeper strategy, more intense competition, and exciting new features that will keep every manager on their toes. Every match you play, every deal you strike, every decision you make will have a ripple effect on your path to the top.

**This is the season we’ve all been waiting for, so get ready for November 5🔥**

---

### TONS of new features!

The core features that define MFL and bring our vision to life, like player progression and promotion/relegation will make their debut in Season 1 🤩  
On top of that, we’re introducing exciting enhancements to make your management experience smoother, more enjoyable, and more strategic.

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*flxzpL9GWW1Yy2rdV9aZxw.png&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

We’ll provide more detailed explanations on each of these topics in the coming days. Here’s an overview in the meantime 👇

### 📈 Player Progression: Nurture Future Legends

One of the most anticipated features is the introduction of **player progression**. Players will no longer be static entities; they’ll grow, develop skills, and evolve based on your decisions. Watch as your players grow in real-time, with attributes that evolve through team training and match experience.

### ⏳ Aging & Retirement: Time Waits for no Man

Season 1 brings a whole new challenge to MFL with **Aging & Retirement**. Your players won’t last forever — each season, they’ll get older, and as they approach retirement, you’ll need to make tough choices. Will you rely on their experience for one last push, or start grooming the next generation of talent? With their future hanging in the balance, the pressure is on to find the right moment to say goodbye to your legends.

### 🔃 Promotion and Relegation: The Stakes Have Never Been Higher

Season 1 will introduce a **promotion and relegation system** to intensify the competition. **Climb the Divisions** and prove your managerial prowess by leading your club to the top tier. Fight for survival in a league where every match matters.

[**🔗FULL GUIDE TO PROMOTION/RELEGATION**](https://blog.playmfl.com/your-guide-to-promotion-and-relegation-836dea00be82)

### 📊 The MFL Development Center: Free Agents Need Love, Too

Agents now have an exciting **new option for player progression: the MFL Development Center**. This provides an alternative progression path for players. If securing a club contract is challenging, enroll them in the Development Center to hone their skills and improve their ratings in exchange for $MFL (unless you won a Free Pass from the Wheel!). It’s a straightforward way to ensure players continue to grow and stay competitive in the ever-evolving MFL landscape.

[**🔗LEARN MORE ON THE DEDICATED ARTICLE**](https://blog.playmfl.com/introducing-the-mfl-development-center-nurturing-free-agents-6c68f9ae2d97)

### 📃 Contract Clauses: Fine Tune Your Offers

Want to ensure your rising star gets the playing time they deserve? Club owners can now include specific conditions in contracts — like minimum match appearances — giving agents more leverage when negotiating deals. Plus, contract offers will now come with an expiry date, and owners will have the option to request a final confirmation before the deal is sealed.

---

### 💰 Increased Rewards: Play Big, Win Bigger

To match the heightened competition, we’re **increasing the rewards compared to the Alpha Seasons!** Get more $MFL than ever in competition, and use it to redeem players, send players in the Development Center, or even enter Rapid Play tournaments!
