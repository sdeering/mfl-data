### Join the Breakaway: Goodbye, Alpha!

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*LWWOlE8HFwnSEzycNfHrcw.png&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

As we gear up for the highly anticipated launch of Season 1, we’re excited to introduce **The Breakaway**, a special month of fun designed to keep the energy flowing, reward our loyal community, and set the stage for what’s coming. Think of it as a **mini-season** with a fresh twist — packed with activities, tournaments, and other opportunities to win prizes until the next full season officially kicks off 🎁

*Here’s what’s coming up, and all you need to know about* ***The Breakaway:***

### 🚀 What is The Breakaway?

Unlike the traditional seasons, **The Breakaway** focuses on fast-paced competitions and shorter events, **without your trusty Leagues or IMFF Cup**. This is our way of giving you something rewarding and different while we prepare for S1 👀

---

### ⚽️ COMPETE

#### 🥇 Breakaway Leagues

Open to all, the Breakaway Leagues are 8-team leagues with daily matches. You will be entered with teams of similar strength, with rewards in $MFL for the top 2 and a **Breakaway Wheel** ticket for each win earned 🎫

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*tD-bD68i0bHKX4b9bEcaLA.jpeg&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

#### 🏆 The Breakaway Cup

Also open to every club is the Breakaway Cup! This cup follows the same format as the IMFF Cup, with a twist: Grab a **Breakaway** **Wheel** ticket for each win, and make the quarterfinals to bag some $MFL.

#### ✨ Alpha Champions

64-team single-elimination tournaments reserved exclusively to previous champions from the League and IMFF Cup. Again, each win will grant you a Breakaway Wheel ticket. The winner of each bracket will snag a brand new Club License up to the Silver Division!

#### ⚡️ Rapid Play

Enter quick-fire tournaments with instant gratification! Put your $MFL on the line or join a **Freeroll** and take home a share of the prize pool. Breakaway Wheel tickets to be won, too!

---

### 🎡 SPIN THE BREAKAWAY WHEEL AND WIN

#### 🎫 The Breakaway Wheel

Whether you’re competing in the **Breakaway Leagues**, **The Breakaway Cup,** or **Rapid Play** tournaments, you’ll have a chance to **collect tickets** that allow you to spin the Breakaway Wheel! Give it a whirl and you could win Players, Packs, or $MFL 🎁

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*brrX6roSq5DMzwSUg5ffcw.png&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

#### ❤️ A little token of appreciation for our seasoned owners

If you’re an MFL OG, we’ll add a little something to your profile sometime soon.

---

As you can see , there’s plenty to play for in October. We’ll have exciting news about what comes *after* The Breakaway very soon so stay tuned. Until then, we hope you’ll enjoy all the events we’ve prepared for you. Be sure to enter your club when registrations — and the transfer window — open up this Friday!
