### Referral Program: Squad up & Score

We’re rolling out the red carpet for the MFL community with the launch of our brand-new Referral Program! It’s time to rally your football enthusiast friends and bring them into the world of MFL. By referring your mates, you don’t just get to share the thrill of competition with them; you also score some fantastic rewards.

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*D2Y_sU_zkpd0Ol0EyVOEuQ.png&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

### Here’s the Game Plan:

When you bring new managers into the MFL universe, both you and your referees score rewards. Here’s how you can make the assist and reap the benefits:

1. **Share Your Unique Link:** Kick things off by sharing your personalized referral link with your friends. It’s their golden ticket into the MFL world.
2. **They Sign Up, You Both Win:** When your referee signs up and spends $17 on packs within 30 days, you both receive a common player to boost your respective squads. Win-win! Refer more friends to unlock even better rewards 👇

[**Get My Unique Referral Link**](https://app.playmfl.com/settings)

### The Rewards Keep Coming 🎁

The more friends you introduce to MFL, the greater the rewards. With our tiered milestone system, the incentives get sweeter as your referral count climbs. Here’s a sneak peek at the potential haul you’ll get instead of a common player when you reach the following milestones:

- **3 Referrals:** Net a Starter Pack
- **5 Referrals:** Bag an Uncommon Player
- **10 Referrals:** Secure a Standard Pack
- **20 Referrals:** Grab a Rare Player
- **30 Referrals:** Unlock a Rare Pack

### Keeping It Fair and Square:

We’re all about fair play, which is why there’s a cap at 30 referrals per user. The program is designed to be a genuine boost to our community. Multi-accounting and submitting fake referrals is strictly prohibited, and will result in account suspension.

### Your Move, Managers:

Your friends are just a few clicks away from joining you on the virtual pitch, and those rewards are waiting to be claimed.

Keep an eye on your progress directly on the MFL platform where you can track your referrals and see how close you are to the next milestone. Share your love for MFL, invite friends, and let the games (and gains) begin!

Remember, this is more than a game; it’s a community, a journey, a passion for football that we share. Let’s make it bigger and better, together.
