### Closing out 2023 and S0.3: Holiday Heroes!

### Celebrating League Champions and Looking Forward

A Round of Applause for League and IMFF Cup Winners 👏👏👏

As we wrap up the leagues, it’s time to extend a hearty congratulations to all the champs. The journey through the ups and downs of a long season was nothing short of epic. Clinching the top spot in these incredibly tight competitions is a testament to your skill, dedication, and strategic prowess. Well done!

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*JBsjv37g5fZ9nZEUf435Dw.png&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

We also want to express our deepest gratitude to all the dedicated managers who participated this season. Your enthusiasm and feedback have been invaluable, especially as we launched new features like **Captains** and the **updated Energy/Fatigue** system. This season was a blast, and it’s all thanks to you ❤️

### Key Dates to Watch Out For

**Transfer Window Reopening — December 21st to 29th**

Heads up, managers! The transfer window is swinging open again on December 21st. This is a golden opportunity to review your squad.  
Whether it’s renewing contracts or bringing in fresh talent, these strategic decisions could be crucial for your success in the season’s grand finale. Remember, the window only stays open until December 29th, so make your moves count!

**Boxing Day Draw — December 26th**

Have your lottery tickets at the ready? The much-anticipated Boxing Day Draw is happening on December 26th! Join us on the Discord server as we spin the wheel for some thrilling prizes.

**Boxing Day Clashes — December 26–27th**

For the fortunate ones who found a ticket in their Advent Calendar, brace yourselves for the Boxing Day Clashes! Get set to compete in these 8-team mini knockout tournaments, where 200 MFL Points are up for grabs. Managers with multiple clubs, your highest-ranked club will take the stage. Keep an eye on your schedule — the clash is on!

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*ViSTLwK7XE8SELNB3ycBVg.png&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

### Holiday Heroes — A Festive Finale to Season 0.3

A Tournament with a Festive Twist

We’re excited to announce the Holiday Heroes Tournament, a festive extravaganza to wrap up Season 0.3! This competition, akin to the Pre-Season Tournament in format, promises to be a holiday spectacle. Here’s what to expect:

- Clubs will be split into 32-team Tournaments based on the strength of the top 11 players they have under contract.
- New Formations Unlocked: All ten new formations will be available for use during the tournament. Time to experiment and outmaneuver your opponents!
- Special Scoring Challenge: In a fun twist, the first 5 teams to score in the 20th and 24th minutes of the same match win a Standard Pack.   
  ***NB: If only one team does it, we’ll turn it into a Rare Pack!***

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*I-QDg7bSdDi8aiQqZX0SSA.png&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

### Ready to Make Your Mark?

As we bid adieu to an exhilarating year, these upcoming events and the Holiday Heroes Tournament are the perfect opportunities to showcase your managerial acumen. Let’s end the year with excitement, competition, and a whole lot of fun. Here’s to making the final moments of the year unforgettable!
