### An All-New 2D Match Experience is Coming!

We are thrilled to announce that starting on September 18, MFL managers will have access to a much-anticipated and brand-new 2D match simulation experience! As promised, this major update brings the entire pitch to life, allowing you to see all 22 players in action 🔥

### What to Expect

Our new 2D match simulation comes packed with new features, bringing you closer to the action than ever before.

- **Complete Pitch View**: Watch every player on the field as they move and make decisions in real-time.
- **Multiple Viewing Modes**: Choose how you want to experience the game with options including Highlights, Extended Highlights, or even the Full Match in 2D.
- **Skippable Timeline & Customizable Speed**: Replaying a match? Skip to the crucial moments that matter most to you!

Plus, we’re cooking up an exclusive competition that will allow its lucky participants to be the first to see their teams on the pitch with the new match experience! More below.

---

### First Look Faceoff: Be the First to Experience the New Simulation

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*1IkNl_p51glcVG5aXMth6Q.png&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

To celebrate the launch, we’re hosting an exclusive tournament where 64 lucky managers will get the first taste of the 2D match simulation. Starting today, you’ll have the opportunity to win your spot and be the first to see your squad in action on the pitch.

#### **How to Secure Your Spot:**

- **Qualifiers**: Compete in daily qualifying events from September 5 to 11. Each day offers a new opportunity to claim your place in the tournament.
- **Giveaways**: Keep an eye out for giveaways from us and partnering content creators on Twitter/X and Discord!

**The tournament will take place from September 12 to 17 and will feature 64 teams. Even if you’re not part of it, you’ll be able to follow the action ⚽️**
