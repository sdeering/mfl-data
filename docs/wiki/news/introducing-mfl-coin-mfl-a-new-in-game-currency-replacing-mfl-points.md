# Introducing MFL Coin ($MFL): A New In-Game Currency Replacing MFL Points

LucasApril 25, 20243 min read

Goodbye MFL Points, Hello MFL Coin ($MFL)!

---

### Introducing MFL Coin ($MFL): A New In-Game Currency Replacing MFL Points

#### Goodbye MFL Points, Hello MFL Coin ($MFL)!

We’re thrilled to announce a transformative update: $MFL, a new in-game currency set to replace MFL Points at the end of this season, designed to enhance how you play, earn, and spend in the MFL universe.

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*XavzAcMbXLvKMe8FKm1xzA.png&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

### What’s $MFL?

Earned exclusively through gameplay, $MFL is a non-tradable currency designed to simplify and enhance your interaction within the MFL ecosystem. **It is not a token and cannot be purchased or traded.**

More below!

---

### Why $MFL?

The current MFL Points system has served us well, but as MFL grows, we need a system that can better support the dynamic and evolving needs of our community. $MFL is designed to be more intuitive and integrated, offering a clearer understanding of value within the game:

#### 📈 Elevated Reward Experience

Feedback from users indicated that MFL Points felt like mere in-game points with no real value or utility in the MFL universe. We wanted to change that perception. This change allows us to rebrand MFL Points in favor of something that feels more tangible. Transitioning to MFL Coin also fosters a more rewarding gameplay experience thanks to the larger figures, increasing the perceived value of rewards while granting us more flexibility for the planning of competition rewards.

#### 🛒 More Utility

As before, you can use $MFL to purchase single player packs in the MFL Store. What’s new, though, is that **you’ll also be able to buy other packs, via drops previously only accessible with real money, using your hard-earned $MFL!**

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*g6ohp6IhXOMU6DrUeIggJQ.png&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

#### 💲Tangible Worth

**800 $MFL equates to $1 (USD) in value in the MFL Store**. As an example, you will be able to buy a pack worth $20 on the MFL Store for 16,000 $MFL.  
This valuation is based on the prices of packs in the MFL store, and makes it easier for managers to understand the real impact of their earnings and expenditures, guiding their strategic decisions within the game.

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*QI3nVSR_J2kEuWT1fSRLOQ.png&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

---

### What’s Changing?

#### 🏷 New Store Prices

After analyzing market trends, we found our previous price ratios were still relevant but needed a slight adjustment to better reflect market dynamics. We’ve adjusted the prices of the single-player packs available in the MFL Store as per the image below.

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*4ywMOFTBxkV8aj23BsYqmA.png&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

#### ⚖ ️Balance update

We wanted to give our early supporters a preferential rate ❤️Even though we only multiplied the price of packs in the Store by factors between 10–14.9x (see the above image), **we have decided to multiply current MFL Points balances by 15.** That means if you have 100 MFL Points now, you’ll have 1,500 $MFL once we switch at the end of this season. In short, your purchasing power just got a boost!

#### 🏆Rewards

The methods for earning $MFL remain the same — you can earn it through active participation in the MFL ecosystem: through competitions, as rewards from player contracts, and by engaging in community events.

As you may already know, rewards are significantly scaled down during Alpha seasons. They will be reviewed regularly to ensure the best gameplay experience, sustainability of the game, and responsiveness to market trends.

---

This exciting change kicks in at the end of Season 0.5 — keep a close watch on your balances! Thank you for being a vital part of our community. We can’t wait to see how you leverage MFL Coin.

See you on the pitch.

*– The MFL Team*
