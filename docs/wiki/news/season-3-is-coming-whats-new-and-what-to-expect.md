### Season 3 Is Coming: What’s New and What to Expect!

It’s almost time for Season 3 in MFL, and we couldn’t be more excited to share what’s coming your way. With exciting new features like Player Instructions and suspensions, this season is set to raise the bar 🔥

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*mlMo1cgp5ohLMVcJ79P1mQ.png&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

Let’s dive into everything you need to know about Season 3, including the key dates and new features!

---

### 📅 Key Dates for Season 3

Here’s the timeline to keep your season on track. Don’t miss a single deadline!

- **Transfer Window Opens:** Jan 28
- **Club Registration Starts:** Jan 28
- **Player Pack Drops:** Jan 29 and Feb 19
- **Season 3 Matchday 1:** Feb 5

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*p2ltyiuTcqDeozwnHY1ZYA.gif&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

We’ve recently launched an [**interactive calendar**](https://app.playmfl.com/competitions?calendar=true) to make it easy to track all the important events. It even shows dates in your local time zone, so there’s no confusion. Check it out now on the **Competitions** page!

[**>> View the Interactive Calendar**](https://app.playmfl.com/competitions?calendar=true)

---

### ✨ What’s New in Season 3

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*AcnymoeslNLcsVP9FmqesQ.png&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

#### Player Instructions

Shape your strategy by giving individual players tailored roles on the pitch to execute your vision. Whether you’re assigning position freedom, passing risk, or specialized attacking and defensive roles, this new feature gives you the control to fine-tune your tactics like never before.

[**>> Learn more about Player Instructions**](https://blog.playmfl.com/taking-tactics-to-the-next-level-introducing-player-instructions-8fa0935ded17)

---

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*F3PG49X41mWFT_49lEFUJA.png&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

#### Suspensions

Manage your squad wisely — players who rack up cards will now face time on the sidelines. This adds a new layer of strategy to your season, making discipline and squad depth more important than ever.

[**>> Learn more about Suspensions**](https://blog.playmfl.com/suspension-rules-in-mfl-stay-sharp-on-discipline-9c7468aacc9c)

---

#### Stadium Names

Pick a name for your club’s own stadium and play home games, well, at home! Add a personal touch to your club’s identity and give your fans a home ground to cheer from.

#### Playmaker Trophy

A small but exciting addition to reward teamwork and creativity on the pitch: We’re introducing the Playmaker Trophy to celebrate the players with the most assists throughout the season in both the Cups and Leagues.

---

### Ready for Season 3?

With these exciting updates and an action-packed schedule ahead, we can’t wait to see how you and your clubs adapt to the new challenges. Make sure you’re prepared — register your club, fine-tune your squad, and mark your calendars for the biggest season yet.
