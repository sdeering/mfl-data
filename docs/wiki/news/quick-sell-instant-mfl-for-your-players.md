# Quick Sell: Instant $MFL for Your Players

MFL TeamJune 23, 20262 min read

Every squad ends up with players you are never going to use. Veterans past their best, depth you will never field, cards you picked up along the way and moved on from. Until now, the only way out was the Marketplace: list the player, set a price, and wait for a buyer.

Quick Sell changes that. It lets you sell eligible players straight back to MFL for an instant $MFL payout. No listing, no waiting. One click, the $MFL lands in your balance, and the player leaves your agency for good.

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fd13e14gtps4iwl.cloudfront.net%2Fu%2Fcontent%2Fhf_20260623_082031_2ad8f0da-e59d-45cc-996b-cd98fb15015a-2-1782221782905.png&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

## How it works

1. Open a player you own, or select several at once from My Players.
2. Select "Quick Sell" from the context menu. You see the exact $MFL Quick Sell value for each one.
3. Confirm, and the $MFL is credited to you instantly.

You can Quick Sell a single player or batch up to **50 at once**. Because the action is final, you type a short confirmation before it goes through.

## Which players are eligible

You can Quick Sell a player when:

- You own the player.
- The player is **not under an active contract**.
- The player **has not retired**.
- The player has **not announced their retirement**.

There is no age limit on its own. A young prospect and a seasoned veteran are both eligible, right up until retirement is on the horizon. Quick Sell also pauses during the season rollover.

## How the payout is calculated

Your $MFL value comes down to two things: the player's overall rating (OVR) and their age.

- **Higher OVR pays more.**
- \*\*Younger players carry a premium, so the same player is worth more at 16 than at 22.
- **There is a floor of 1 $MFL**, so a Quick Sell always returns something.

## It is final

Quick Sell is permanent. Once you confirm, the player leaves your agency and is taken out of circulation. There is no undo, and you cannot buy the player back afterwards. Think of it as releasing a player for good, not parking them on the bench.

Every Quick Sell permanently removes a player from the ecosystem, essentially putting an end to their career.

When you are ready to trim your squad, Quick Sell turns the players you are done with into $MFL you can spend on the ones you actually want.
