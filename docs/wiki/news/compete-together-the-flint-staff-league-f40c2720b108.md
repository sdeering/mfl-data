# Compete Together: The Flint Staff League

LucasFebruary 17, 20262 min read

If you’re a Flint club registering for Season 12, you’ve got a choice: regular leagues or the new Flint Staff League.

---

### Compete Together: The Flint Staff League

If you’re a Flint club registering for Season 12, you’ve got a choice: **regular leagues or the new Flint Staff League.**

Not both. One or the other.

The Staff League is a new format launching with the Club Staff feature. You bring in a friend who didn’t play Season 11, register together, and compete in 20-team leagues with 38 matches over 22 days.

It’s collaborative management at high intensity. And it’s got some serious perks.

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*Z6gLUe6OTgwwyEZG_Iivsg.png&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

### The Format

#### 2️⃣0️⃣ 20-team leagues

First time we’ve ever run this size. 38 matches packed into the same 22-day season as regular Flint, which means most days you’re playing twice.

That’s a lot of football. Too much for one person to handle perfectly?   
Bring some help in — someone on tactics while you manage energy, someone scouting opponents while you handle rotations. This format rewards delegation.

#### ⬜️⬜️ Commons only

And it’s commons-only. Everyone’s working with the same tier of talent, so success comes down to management and coordination.

### Why Join a Staff League?

#### 🚀 More Progression

**20% boost on Match XP.** Your commons progress 20% faster from playing matches than in regular leagues. That’s massive for player development.

#### ⚡️ Promotion to Spark still on the line

**You can still promote.** It’s harder than regular Flint (between 2.5% and 5% of clubs will promote), but top 4 finishers from each league enter a playoff tournament for promotion spots.

#### 🎉 More fun

Playing with your crew, coordinating across 38 matches, competing in a format nobody’s seen before.

#### 🎁 Win your mate a Club License

Oh, and if you win the whole playoff tournament? One of your staff members gets a club license for Season 13! 🔥

### How To Enter

#### 1️⃣ Have a new or returning user in your staff

You need a staff member who didn’t participate in Season 11 — could be someone new to MFL or someone who played before and stepped away.

#### 2️⃣ Have them register for the Staff League

They register your club for the competition (they need “Manage Competitions” permission).

⚠️ I**f the staff member who registered leaves before season ends**, you’re out of playoff contention. Bring someone who’s truly committed for the whole season!  
⚠️ New and returning staff members **can only register and participate in a Flint Staff League with ONE club**.

### The Choice

- **Regular Flint:** 12-team leagues, 22 matches, standard progression, higher chance to promote.
- **Staff League:** 20-team leagues, 38 matches, commons-only, +20% Match XP boost, harder to promote, collaborative format.

You choose at registration. Registration opens with Season 12.
