### Time to Kick Off Season 2 ⚽

Season 2 begins today, and we’ve got an exciting mix of new features, improvements, and tweaks to take MFL to the next level.

#### 🔄 Pre-Contract Agreements Will Return

You’ve already had a taste of the new **Pre-Contract Agreements**, a feature that lets you plan ahead by locking in players for next season. These agreements will go live again **after Matchday 17**, giving you plenty of time to prepare for Season 3 while the current one enters its final chapter!

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*-ce2vBB-_tdSKgq3VRHfVQ.png&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

#### 🚀 A New & Improved Match Engine

We’re continuing to refine the match experience, and Season 2 introduces some key upgrades to make the action on the pitch even more realistic:

- **Referees now visible**: Spot the officials on the pitch as they oversee the action.
- **Woodwork drama**: Players can now hit the posts and crossbar — more heart-in-mouth (dare I say, butt-clenching?) moments incoming!
- **Enhanced behavior**: Player movements and decisions have been improved for better realism and flow.

Get ready for matches to feel more immersive than ever before.

---

### 📅 Your Season 2 Timeline

To keep things dynamic and predictable, Season 2 will follow our new 6-week season format. With Rollover Day now complete, here are the key dates:

- **December 18**: Player Pack Drop
- **December 25**: Leagues Kick Off
- **January 8**: Midseason Transfer Window & Drop
- **January 21–22**: IMFF Cup Final & Final League Matchday
- **January 24–26**: Promotion Playoffs

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*949fgS6TxsEfdEZtK9Ohqw.png&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

### 💰 Introducing Reward Day

From Season 2 onwards, we’re adding a dedicated **Reward Day** to ensure smoother and more transparent $MFL distribution.

#### How It Works:

- **When**: Rewards will be distributed **after the last league matchday**.
- **Snapshot**: A snapshot will be taken on Reward Day, capturing the club owners and agents of players **under contract at that time**.

⚠️**Important change: Playoff matches will no longer count toward the minimum matches clause in contracts.**
