# The Pack Marketplace Is Live!

LucasSeptember 8, 20252 min read

A new chapter for MFL just dropped: packs are now tradable.

---

### The Pack Marketplace Is Live!

A new chapter for MFL just dropped: **packs are now tradable.**

From today, managers can list their packs for sale on the marketplace — or buy them directly from other managers. That means more liquidity, faster rewards, and a brand-new layer of strategy built around one of the most exciting assets in the game.

![](https://news.playmfl.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmiro.medium.com%2Fv2%2Fresize%3Afit%3A800%2F1*6ur2L8-I33WBrLdy7FHtWw.png&w=1920&q=75&dpl=dpl_7ePanGkvNxQrwXZwv838j6Nd5djz)

### Trade the Mystery 🎲

Not every pack needs to be opened. You can now sell them unopened, turning rewards into instant cash. For the buyer, the suspense is the prize — ripping open a pack that could change their club’s future.

### Packs When You Need Them ⚡

New to MFL and struggling to fill your squad? Or just want to grab fresh players without battling the transfer window? Now you can pick up packs instantly from other managers, right in the marketplace.

### Fees That Keep It Fair 🏷️

For the first week of launch, all pack sales carry a **5% fee**.

#### After that:

- Reward packs **stay at 5%**.
- **Drop packs move to 20%**

Why the difference? Reward packs are earned through playing and competing, so they should remain straightforward to trade on the marketplace.   
Drop packs, on the other hand, are limited-time sales events — and the higher fee is there to discourage quick reselling purely out of speculation.

### How to List a Pack 🎮

Selling packs works just like players or clubs:

1. Head to your **Inventory tab** in *My HQ*.
2. Choose the pack you want to sell.
3. It’ll appear in the new **Packs tab** on the marketplace.

Each listing shows the pack’s **Release Season** — crucial info, since players age one year per season. For example, a pack marked **Release Season: S4** (and we’re now in Season 8) contains players who’ve already aged four years.

---

Packs are now a liquid, tradable asset that gives managers more ways to play, more ways to strategize, and more ways to win!
